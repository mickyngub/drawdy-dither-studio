# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release

- upload an image, or drop or paste one, and turn it into a halftone dot field
- dots or pixels, five dither modes, circle or square dots, and a pixel style that can keep the image's own colours
- sliders for grid, cell and dot size, vignette, posterize, levels, gamma, floor, ceiling and softness, with ink and paper colours
- place the result in the middle of your view at 1x or 2x
- settings are remembered between sessions
