# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release

- turn a board selection into a halftone dot field, from the Dither Studio button or the right-click menu
- dots or pixels, five dither modes, circle or square dots, and a pixel style that can keep the source's own colours
- sliders for grid, cell and dot size, vignette, posterize, levels, gamma, floor, ceiling and softness, with ink and paper colours
- drop or paste an image into the panel to dither it too
- place the result beside the selection or in its place, at 1x or 2x
- settings are remembered between sessions
